# Client Handover

## Handover checklist

- [ ] Business owner and technical owner identified.
- [ ] Onboarding and offboarding workflows approved.
- [ ] Department-to-group mapping validated.
- [ ] License mapping and availability reviewed.
- [ ] MFA / Conditional Access policy reviewed and tested.
- [ ] Required Graph permissions documented and approved.
- [ ] Audit log location, access, and retention agreed.
- [ ] Mailbox, OneDrive, SharePoint, and legal-hold process documented.
- [ ] Failure handling and escalation contact documented.
- [ ] Operators trained and scripts peer-reviewed.
- [ ] Rollback/recovery considerations documented.
- [ ] Production readiness approval recorded.

## Operational ownership

Document who may run each script, how approvals are obtained, where logs are stored, who reviews failures, and how the scripts are updated. Do not deploy the lab scripts directly to production.
